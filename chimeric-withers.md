# Chimeric Withers

A Minecraft mod that allows you to graft additional heads onto a wither to provide new abilities. For example: 
* Adding a blaze head will allow the wither to shoot fireballs to set the player on fire.
* Adding a bee head could allow the wither to shoot poison darts
* Creeper head would increase explosive power of the wither skulls that shoot at the player
* Enderman head could allow the wither to steal blocks from around the target or to teleport
* Zombie Piglin head could grant "hive mind" aggression, allowing the wither to "command" any nearby mobs to attack, even normally passive mobs like animals
* Slime head could make the wither skulls bouncy or could make them coat an area in slime slowing the target
* Dragon head could make the wither skull explosions leave behind an area of dragons breath

Multiple heads can be added to a single wither structure to grant multiple new abilities. There should be rewards for fighting these harder wither variants, but I've not managed to come up with an idea for that.
