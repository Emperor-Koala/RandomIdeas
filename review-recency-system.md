# Review Recency System/Service

A service that acts as a business review platform similar to yelp, but with a rating system similar to Reddit where more recent reviews are weighted heavier than older ones. Rather than a handfull of negative reviews forever weighing down a business, particularly if that business comes under new and better management, those reviews will eventually "age out," allowing businesses to recover. When combined with newer more positive reviews, they will be included less and less in the final rating. I think ideally this could interface with other review platforms to provide a complete and honest accounting of reviews, such as combining all the reviews from Google and Yelp and TripAdvisor and others, then applying appropriate age-weights to those reviews before calculating the final rating.
